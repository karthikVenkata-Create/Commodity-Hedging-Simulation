## Round 2 decision

Choice: Option B - Hedge 60% (60,000 units) at a fixed swap price of $115.

Rationale: I decided to hedge 60% of our production to stay on the safe side. The main goal is to make sure we can easily cover our $3 million in operating costs and $1 million in interest payments even if prices fall. Locking in most of our output at $115 gives steady cash flow and prevents a major loss that could lead to bankruptcy and a $20 million penalty.

I left 40% unhedged just in case the market improves, so the company can still gain from a potential price increase. Overall, this is a conservative and low-risk approach that focuses on financial stability and long-term survival instead of short-term gains.

Final decision: Hedge 60,000 units at $115 fixed, to protect the company's financial stability and minimize bankruptcy risk while keeping a small chance to benefit if prices rise.
