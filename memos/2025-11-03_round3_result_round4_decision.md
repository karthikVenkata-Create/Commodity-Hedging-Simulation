Choice: Option B - Hedge 60% (60,000 units) at a fixed swap price of $85.

Rationale: For this round, I'm choosing to hedge 60% of the production. The main reason is to make sure the company is protected if prices fall a lot. By locking in $85 on 60,000 units, we are basically guaranteeing enough revenue to stay above the $5M minimum and avoid the big $20M bankruptcy hit.

At the same time, I don't want to hedge everything, because if the market price ends up being higher than $85, we want to be able to benefit from it. Keeping the remaining 40% unhedged lets us still take advantage of any price increase. So this choice keeps us safe on the downside while still giving us a chance to benefit if prices go up.

Final decision: Hedge 60,000 units at $85 fixed.
