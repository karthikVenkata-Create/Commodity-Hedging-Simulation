## Round 3 decision

Choice: Option B - Hedge 70% (70,000 units) at a fixed swap price of $135.

Rationale: I chose to hedge 70% of production to protect the company from a potential price drop that could push revenue below the $5 million minimum and trigger a $20 million bankruptcy penalty.

Leaving the remaining 30% unhedged allows us to still benefit if prices rise, so we don't completely give up upside. This balances risk and reward while focusing on long-term financial stability rather than risky short-term gains.

Final decision: Hedge 70,000 units at $135 fixed, to protect the company from large downside risk while keeping some exposure for potential price gains.
